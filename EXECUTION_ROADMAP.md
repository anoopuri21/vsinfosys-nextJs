## 1. Operating Principle

The website and the search-growth system are one product.

VS Infosys will not first build a beautiful website and add SEO later. Search intent, page architecture, content, semantic HTML, performance budgets, internal links, structured data, local signals, analytics and automated monitoring must influence every phase from the beginning.

The target is:

> **A cinematic website that attracts clients visually and an autonomous SEO operating system that continuously discovers, checks, measures and prioritizes growth opportunities.**

### Two equal quality bars

#### Experience quality

- Premium cinematic design
- Clear messaging
- High conversion intent
- Responsive and accessible interaction
- Memorable brand presence

#### Search quality

- Crawlable and indexable pages
- Strong local and service relevance
- Helpful original content
- Healthy Core Web Vitals
- Clear internal architecture
- Accurate structured data
- Continuous measurement and improvement

Neither quality bar may be sacrificed for the other.

---

## 2. Important Reality About “Fully Automatic Ranking”

Google ranking cannot be guaranteed or made completely autonomous. Google states that following Search Essentials makes a site more likely to appear, but indexing and ranking are not guaranteed. Search changes may take from hours to several months to show an effect.

Automation can reliably handle:

- technical validation;
- sitemap generation and submission;
- metadata and schema templates;
- broken-link detection;
- Core Web Vitals collection;
- Search Console data collection;
- ranking-opportunity detection;
- content-decay detection;
- internal-link recommendations;
- alerts and scheduled reports;
- content brief assistance; and
- publishing workflows after approval.

Automation cannot safely replace:

- business positioning;
- first-hand expertise;
- fact verification;
- final content judgment;
- genuine reviews;
- Google Business Profile verification;
- relationship-based links and mentions;
- original research; or
- strategic decisions after major ranking changes.

Google’s spam policies specifically prohibit scaled content created primarily to manipulate rankings, including large volumes of low-value generative AI pages. Automated Google-result scraping for rank checking is also prohibited. Therefore, VS Infosys will use an **80% automation / 20% human approval model**, not unsupervised auto-publishing.

### Automation principle

> Automate repetition, detection and validation. Keep truth, expertise and publication accountability human-controlled.

# PHASE 1 — Search Opportunity Research

## Objective

Determine what potential clients actually search, which queries indicate commercial intent, how difficult the market is and where VS Infosys can create a differentiated answer.

## Tasks

### P1-T01 — Search-market definition

Separate the market into:

- Delhi NCR local-intent searches
- Pan-India commercial searches
- Service-specific searches
- Platform-specific searches
- Problem and comparison searches
- AI and emerging-technology searches

### P1-T02 — Seed topic research

Research candidates around:

- web design company Delhi;
- website development company Delhi;
- dynamic website development;
- WordPress development Delhi;
- Shopify development Delhi;
- e-commerce website development;
- SEO services Delhi;
- digital marketing services Delhi;
- content writing and graphic design;
- video generation services;
- AI implementation and automation;
- app development Delhi; and
- domain and hosting support.

These are research seeds, not automatically approved targets.

### P1-T03 — Intent classification

Every query will be classified as:

- **Transactional:** wants to hire or request a quote
- **Commercial investigation:** compares agencies, platforms, prices or approaches
- **Informational:** wants guidance or education
- **Local:** includes Delhi/NCR or “near me” behavior
- **Navigational:** seeks VS Infosys directly

### P1-T04 — Competitor/SERP review

For each priority cluster, record:

- result types;
- ranking page type;
- title and heading patterns;
- content depth;
- local pack presence;
- media formats;
- common weak points;
- backlink-worthy assets;
- page speed and UX quality; and
- opportunities to provide a better answer.

### P1-T05 — Query-to-page map

Assign one primary search intent to one canonical page. Avoid several pages competing for the same query.

### P1-T06 — Opportunity scoring

Score each candidate topic using:

- commercial relevance;
- local relevance;
- expected competition;
- business priority;
- ability to provide original value;
- conversion potential; and
- supporting-content potential.

## Automation

- Import approved keyword data into a structured research collection
- Normalize duplicates
- Cluster related terms
- Detect page/query conflicts
- Generate first-draft SERP comparison tables

## Human approval

Final keyword selection requires strategic judgment. Search volume alone must not decide the roadmap.

## Deliverables

- Search opportunity workbook
- Competitor matrix
- Commercial-intent map
- Local-intent map
- Priority keyword clusters
- Query-to-page map

## Completion gate

No final URL or page brief is approved without an assigned audience, intent and conversion objective.

- LCP ≤ 2.5s at the 75th percentile
- INP ≤ 200ms at the 75th percentile
- CLS ≤ 0.1 at the 75th percentile
- Production Lighthouse performance target ≥ 90 on representative pages

- Maximum three signature cinematic sequences on the homepage
- No essential text inside canvas or images
- One active canvas/WebGL context maximum
- Hero communicates before enhancement assets load
- No autoplay full-resolution video on mobile
- Two font families maximum
- Limited font weights/files

- Initial JavaScript budget defined and enforced in CI after framework baseline measurement
- Critical above-fold transfer budget defined after the hero prototype
- Image and 3D asset budget per section

- Keep every indexable heading, paragraph, link and FAQ in semantic HTML

# PHASE 7 — Autonomous SEO System

## Objective

Build an internal SEO operations layer that automates repetitive work, detects opportunities and prevents regressions.

## 7.1 Content publishing automation

This workflow can run through WordPress hooks, PHP administration or an external release script depending on the final shared-hosting choice.

On draft/save:

- suggest slug from approved title;
- check duplicate slug;
- preview title and description length;
- require one H1 through the template;
- flag missing summary, image or alt text;
- suggest related services and guides;
- validate canonical target;
- validate robots state;
- generate Open Graph preview;
- generate schema from verified fields;
- flag unsupported schema claims.

On publish:

- regenerate affected static routes;
- update sitemap and accurate `lastmod`;
- refresh internal-link index;
- record publication/version history;
- queue page for crawl/indexation monitoring;
- run automated URL QA.

## 7.2 CI SEO validation

Every deployment should automatically test:

- status codes;
- accidental noindex;
- robots conflicts;
- missing/duplicate titles;
- missing descriptions;
- H1 count;
- canonical validity;
- broken internal links;
- orphan pages;
- image alt rules;
- structured data syntax;
- sitemap validity;
- redirect chains;
- missing social metadata;
- Lighthouse performance/accessibility/SEO budgets.

Critical failures block deployment.

## 7.3 Search Console automation

Use official Search Console APIs to collect:

- clicks;
- impressions;
- CTR;
- average position;
- page-query pairs;
- device split;
- country split;
- sitemap status;
- URL inspection diagnostics within allowed quotas.

### Important API limitation

The URL Inspection API reports Google’s indexed view; it does not request indexing. Google’s general Indexing API is not intended for ordinary service pages—it is restricted to supported content such as job postings and livestream pages. For normal pages, use crawlable links, accurate sitemaps and Search Console-supported processes.

## 7.4 Opportunity engine

Scheduled weekly logic should detect:

- high impressions with low CTR;
- queries ranking approximately positions 4–20;
- pages losing clicks or impressions;
- pages with query cannibalization;
- new queries without a strong landing page;
- guides that should link to a service;
- services missing supporting content;
- pages not indexed after a reasonable period;
- content not reviewed within its defined freshness interval.

The engine creates a prioritized task queue; it does not directly rewrite production pages.

## 7.5 Internal-link recommendation engine

- Index page topics and entities
- Detect relevant source/target pairs
- Suggest natural anchor context
- Reject repetitive exact-match anchors
- Prevent circular or excessive linking
- Require editorial approval before insertion

## 7.6 Technical health monitoring

Daily or scheduled checks:

- uptime;
- SSL expiry;
- sitemap accessibility;
- robots changes;
- canonical changes;
- 404/5xx spikes;
- redirect loops;
- form delivery;
- structured data failures;
- security headers;
- malware/Safe Browsing alerts where available.

## 7.7 Performance monitoring

Collect field Web Vitals using the `web-vitals` library and send them to an approved analytics endpoint.

Track:

- LCP element and route;
- INP interaction target;
- CLS source;
- device/network category;
- p75 over rolling periods;
- release-to-release regression.

Alert when a metric exceeds the agreed threshold or deteriorates significantly.

## 7.8 Content intelligence

Automation may:

- create a brief from approved Search Console queries;
- identify missing subtopics;
- recommend updates;
- check readability and duplication;
- surface outdated dates or claims;
- propose title/meta variants; and
- recommend diagrams or tools.

Automation may not:

- publish without approval;
- invent expertise;
- generate mass pages;
- scrape and paraphrase competitors;
- manufacture links or reviews.

## Deliverables

- SEO publishing panel or release-report interface suited to the selected shared-hosting setup
- deployment validation suite
- Search Console data pipeline
- Automated SEO dashboard
- Opportunity task queue
- Technical alerting
- Web Vitals monitoring
- Internal-link recommendation workflow

## Completion gate

The system must catch a deliberate test regression—such as accidental noindex, broken canonical or missing title—before production deployment.

# PHASE 10 — Launch and Indexation

## Objective

Release the website safely, help search engines discover the canonical pages and verify that real users can convert.

## Launch sequence

1. Freeze content and DNS changes
2. Create backup/rollback point
3. Deploy production build
4. Verify HTTPS, host redirects and status codes
5. Remove staging restrictions only from production
6. Test robots and sitemap
7. Submit sitemap through Search Console
8. Inspect homepage and priority pages
9. Verify structured data
10. Test forms, email and WhatsApp
11. Confirm analytics and Web Vitals
12. Monitor server logs and errors

## First 14 days

- Check indexed page coverage
- Monitor brand queries
- Review crawl errors
- Resolve canonical mismatches
- Check mobile rendering
- Review conversion events
- Monitor field-performance samples as data becomes available

## Important expectation

Sitemap submission supports discovery but does not guarantee indexing. Ranking movement should be assessed over appropriate time windows rather than daily reactions.

# PHASE 11 — First 90-Day Growth Cycle

## Objective

Use actual search and user data to improve pages systematically.

## Days 0–30 — Discovery and stability

- Verify indexation
- Fix crawl/render issues
- Establish brand search presence
- Complete Google Business Profile
- Review early query impressions
- Improve titles where intent mismatch is clear
- Validate conversion tracking

## Days 31–60 — Relevance expansion

- Optimize pages receiving impressions but weak CTR
- Add missing FAQ/supporting explanations
- Strengthen internal links
- Publish the next approved guide
- Improve service-page depth from real enquiry questions
- Review local pack and organic competitors

## Days 61–90 — Authority and conversion

- Release one useful interactive asset
- Begin genuine outreach around that asset
- Refresh weak commercial pages
- Compare query-to-lead quality
- Improve project starter based on drop-off
- Review Core Web Vitals field data
- Plan the next content cluster

## 90-day review

Measure:

- indexed canonical pages;
- non-brand impressions;
- priority-query visibility;
- Delhi/local visibility;
- clicks and CTR;
- organic enquiries;
- enquiry quality;
- service-page engagement;
- Core Web Vitals p75;
- guide-assisted conversions.

Do not evaluate success using total traffic alone.

- Position/impression trend detection
- Indexation queue review
- Broken-link and orphan-page scan
- Core Web Vitals trend report

## Monthly human review

- Approve opportunity backlog
- Review organic lead quality
- Decide content updates
- Validate local profile information
- Review competitor movement
- Approve new content briefs
- Remove or improve underperforming content

## Quarterly strategy review

- Re-run keyword and SERP research
- Re-evaluate service priorities
- Consolidate cannibalizing pages
- Review architecture and internal links
- Audit structured data eligibility
- Review brand/entity consistency
- Plan an original authority asset
- Reassess cinematic performance cost

## Content refresh triggers

Create a review task when:

- impressions fall materially over a sustained period;
- CTR underperforms for a stable position;
- a guide contains outdated platform information;
- a service changes;
- enquiries reveal unanswered questions;
- two pages begin competing for the same query;
- facts, prices or screenshots become outdated.

Automation creates the task and evidence. A person decides the change.

- reason;

# 5. On-Page SEO Standard for Every Indexable Page

Every indexable page must have:

1. One clear search intent
2. One descriptive title
3. One useful meta description
4. One H1
5. Logical H2/H3 structure
6. A direct answer near the beginning
7. Original, useful body content
8. Crawlable contextual internal links
9. Descriptive image alt text where needed
10. Self-referencing canonical
11. Correct robots directive
12. Accurate structured data where eligible
13. Clear author/reviewer information for guides when appropriate
14. Honest update/publication dates
15. A relevant conversion action
16. Good mobile experience
17. Passing performance and accessibility gates

### Homepage-specific target

The homepage should target the brand, broad service proposition and genuine Delhi context. It should not try to rank equally for every detailed service query. Dedicated service pages carry focused commercial intent.

# 7. Search Growth Timeline

There is no honest fixed timeline for first-page rankings. A new or low-authority domain generally needs technical quality, relevant content, local trust, genuine mentions and time.

### Expected operating stages

#### 0–30 days

- Crawl/indexation stability
- Brand-result control
- Google Business Profile foundation
- Early long-tail impressions

#### 1–3 months

- Query discovery
- Long-tail and local relevance growth
- Service-page optimization from real data

#### 3–6 months

- Stronger commercial-cluster visibility
- Content/tool-assisted authority
- More meaningful organic enquiries if execution remains consistent

#### 6–12 months

- Compounding internal-link and topical authority
- Competitive query gains
- Stronger local and service recognition

These are planning windows, not ranking promises.

VS Infosys should not have to choose between an impressive website and a search-performing website.
