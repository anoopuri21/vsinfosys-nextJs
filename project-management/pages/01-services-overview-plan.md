# Selected Page 01 — Services Overview

## Route

`/services/`

## Search intent

Visitors comparing the complete capabilities of a web, technology and digital-growth company in Delhi.

## Primary SEO theme

IT services company Delhi; web design, development, SEO, commerce, content and AI services.

Exact target wording remains subject to verified keyword research. Copy must stay natural and useful.

## Page job

Help a visitor understand the complete VS Infosys offer, choose the correct capability hub and move toward a project conversation.

## Section architecture

1. **Cinematic hero** — “One connected digital partner.”
2. **Outcome map** — five buyer outcomes connected through one spectrum line.
3. **Digital Presence** — web, dynamic websites, WordPress, domain and hosting.
4. **Products & Commerce** — e-commerce, Shopify and customer-facing products.
5. **Growth** — SEO and digital marketing.
6. **Content** — writing, graphics and video generation.
7. **Practical AI** — AI implementation and workflow automation.
8. **Applications** — applications, portals and connected systems.
9. **How capabilities combine** — three example engagement structures without fictional clients.
10. **Build standards** — performance, responsive behavior, SEO, accessibility and maintainability.
11. **Decision guide** — questions that help select a starting point.
12. **Related practical guides** — only real/published resources.
13. **FAQ** — scope, platforms, combined services and next steps.
14. **Project CTA** — route into Start a Project.

## Graphics

- Spectrum capability orbit
- Connected outcome constellation
- Browser structure system
- Commerce path
- Search-intent cluster
- Content prism
- AI workflow
- Application layers

Every graphic explains a relationship. No generic decorative stock artwork.

## Motion

- Hero constellation responds gently to pointer movement
- Outcome line draws as the section enters
- Capability visuals reveal through layered masks
- No long pinned scene on this page
- Reduced-motion mode uses static final states

## On-page requirements

- One H1
- Natural Delhi context in title, introduction and closing—not repeated everywhere
- Crawlable links to all service hubs and detailed service pages
- Unique title and description
- Visible breadcrumbs
- No FAQ schema until production eligibility is reviewed
- No client, result or experience claims

## Conversion

Primary: Start a Project  
Secondary: Select a capability hub
