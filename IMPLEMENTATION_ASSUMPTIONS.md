# Implementation assumptions — VS Infosys

## Confirmed inputs
- Brand: VS Infosys
- Priority services: e-commerce development, website design and development, mobile app development, digital marketing
- Priority industries: real estate, education, tour and travel, e-commerce, NGOs
- Product direction: Next.js frontend, Cloudflare-ready static export, future CMS/API backend
- Brand palette: lavender-led, replacing the previous orange-led direction

## Meaningful assumptions
- Public launch language is English; Hindi/localized routes require a separate content and hreflang strategy.
- Delhi is used as location context, not as a claim of a customer-facing public office.
- No claims about past projects, clients, rankings, awards, testimonials, results, or experience are made until verified evidence is supplied.
- Industry content expresses solution fit, not unverified industry delivery history.
- `hello@vsinfosys.in` is a temporary presentational address and must be confirmed before launch.
- The project starter page intentionally does not submit data yet. A secure Cloudflare Worker/D1/R2 form workflow will be added after domain, mailbox, Access and anti-spam choices are available.
- Keyword examples are research hypotheses. Final keyword-to-page mapping requires Keyword Planner/SERP research before production metadata and copy approval.

## SEO rules
- No `meta keywords` tag or keyword stuffing.
- No mass-generated industry or location pages.
- All indexable routes require a unique search intent, unique content and useful internal links.
- Schema is used only when it reflects visible, verified content.
